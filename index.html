HOW TO READ THIS TOOL

RuleTrace highlights English passages with a small set of extraction rules. It does not use a language model, interpret every exception, verify a website, or make legal decisions. A missing result means “check the full source”, never “no requirement.”

Supported dates and known boundaries

Dates such as “October 26, 2026 at 5:00 PM EDT” or “2026-10-26T21:00Z”. Explicit UTC, GMT, EDT, EST, PDT, PST or numeric offsets (±HH:MM). CST, IST, ET, PT, missing years and missing timezones stay unresolved. Date ranges and unrelated milestones need human review. Paste one condition per line when possible. Candidate categories may overlap. Prize labels are hints; values are never added into a total.
